About us

A palace built for players

Pixel Palace is a new gaming zone in Karachi created by gamers, for gamers. We saw players squeezed into noisy, uncomfortable cafes with slow machines and no sense of community, so we built the place we wanted to play in.

Whether you are grinding ranked matches, trying VR for the first time, or celebrating a birthday with friends, you get fast hardware, comfortable seating, backup power and a team that actually plays games.

Our USP: Karachi's all-in-one gaming palace. Competitive PCs, console lounge, VR, tournaments and a snack bar in one safe, comfortable, community-driven space.

Vision

To become the heart of Pakistan's gaming community, starting in Karachi and growing wherever players want a place to belong.

Mission

To give every gamer a safe, premium and affordable place to play, compete and connect, backed by friendly service and fair play.
